Generative AI Basics02
CONCEPT

What is generative AI?

Generative AI creates new text, images and code from patterns in the data it learned from. And this is very important. Remember that it does not look up the right answer — it predicts a plausible next word.

Good at

First drafts, summaries, translation, brainstorming, reformatting

Be careful with

Recent facts, exact numbers, claims that need a source — a person must check

Four parts of a good prompt

PartWhat it isExample
RoleWho the AI should be“You are an HR manager with 10 years of experience”
ContextBackground and purpose“I’m preparing onboarding material”
TaskWhat to do“Summarize the text below in 5 lines”
FormatShape of the result“As a table, as of 2023”
